# Product

## Promise

Know what Shorts are gaining momentum before everyone else does.

## Core loop

1. Open India-wide momentum.
2. See the few Shorts worth attention.
3. Inspect the deterministic score and observed evidence.
4. Understand why a format is moving.
5. Follow the signal into a category, niche, or creator idea.

## Product boundaries

India is the authoritative MVP region. Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad,
Pune, and Chennai are presented as locked future intelligence, not fake local
data. Historical trends, creator intelligence, competitor radar, and exports
are premium affordances with no fabricated implementation behind them.

The UI always distinguishes observed source fields from interpretation. Sample
fixtures carry a visible label in the dashboard and deep dive.
